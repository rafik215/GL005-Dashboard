💡 Rate Bracket Insights

The breakeven rate is $2.50/total mile at $5.00/gal diesel and 8,000 mi/month. Any load accepted below this rate loses money before you even consider the cost to your dividend. Note that the actual diesel price paid in Apr–May 2026 was $5.21–$5.29/gal, pushing the real breakeven slightly above $2.50 during those months.
Fuel alone is $0.91/mile — the single biggest cost line. At 5.5 MPG and $5.00/gal, fuel consumes 36% of a $2.50/mi load and 23% of a $4.00/mi load. This is why diesel price is the most important external variable to track — a $0.50/gal increase shifts the breakeven rate up by roughly $0.09/mi.
Driver + Admin together ($0.45 of every $1.00 of revenue) mean the effective contribution margin before fixed costs is only $0.55/mi at any rate. At $2.50/mi that leaves $1.375 to cover $1.373 of fixed costs — hence breakeven. At $3.00/mi it leaves $1.65 to cover the same $1.373 — netting $0.277/mi (9.2% margin).
The jump from $2.50 to $3.05/mi (a $0.55/mi rate increase) moves you from breakeven to 10% margin. On a 600-mile load that's $330 more revenue — the difference between a $0 net contribution and a $60 net contribution. GCNU should be using $3.00+/total mile as its target floor, not just anything above fuel cost.
Insurance is the fixed cost you cannot negotiate. At $0.264/total mile combined (GL + BP), it adds up to $2,112/month on 8,000 miles regardless of revenue. On a slow month with fewer miles, the per-mile insurance cost rises — which is why total monthly mileage is the other key lever alongside rate per mile.
